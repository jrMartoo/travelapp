Who We Are

SafiriPesa is a premier Kenyan travel agency dedicated to curating seamless, unforgettable travel experiences. We believe that exploring the world should be as easy as a few taps on your phone.

From the breathtaking savannahs of the Maasai Mara to the pristine beaches of Diani, we handle the logistics so you can focus on the adventure. We are your trusted local partner for global journeys.
